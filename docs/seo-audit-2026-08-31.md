## Closed

**#1 — `llms.txt` review claims. Closed, and then some.** `f21788b` replaced both files
with `scripts/generate-llms.mts`, generating them from `FIXED_ROUTES`. That audit found
what the first pass missed entirely: **136 of 175 price cells were wrong**, nearly all
quoting *less* than checkout charges — Cadaqués €165 against a real €240, Palamós €130
against €185, Montserrat V-Class €145 against €200. Plus meet & greet and the name board
listed as included when both are €5, child seats listed as free at €5 each (and
`llms-full.txt` contradicting itself on the same page), and off-airport waiting stated as
30 minutes against a real 15.

`lib/__tests__/llms-txt.test.ts` is stricter than the test this branch proposed: it bans
*any* rating or review-count claim in the AI-facing files rather than permitting the
verified figure, and it re-checks all 175 price cells against the pricing data. The
hand-edit and test this branch carried are deleted here as superseded.

**#4a — `/fleet` links to its children.** Was zero in-body links to any of the seven
vehicle pages; now all seven, via `components/transfers/HubChildLinks.tsx`, covered by
`lib/__tests__/hub-links.test.ts`.

**#3a — the orphaned entity node is gone.** `#org` no longer appears anywhere. The three
business nodes are down to `#organization` / `#business` / `#website`, consistently.
See "still open" below for the half that did not close.

## Still open

**#2 — sitewide `noindex` still defaults on. Unchanged, and still the top item.**
`next.config.ts:76` is still `if (!isProd)`, gated on `process.env.VERCEL_ENV` read at
build time. Any build outside Vercel's production pipeline ships the whole site with
`X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow`, and it looks completely normal in a browser.
**Please run `curl -I https://www.elitebcn.info/` and confirm no `x-robots-tag` header** —
this still cannot be checked from here, and it is the one finding where being wrong costs
everything else.

**#3b — no page publishes a rating any more.** On 23 Aug, `aggregateRating` appeared on
exactly one page (`/book`). It now appears on **zero of 113**. Removing the orphaned
`#org` node took the rating with it. The verified 5.0★ from 15 Google reviews still sits
in `data/reviews.ts`, and review stars remain a live SERP feature — unlike the FAQ
markup. Attaching it to the `#business` node would put it on all 113 pages.

This is distinct from the `llms.txt` decision. Banning rating claims in the AI-facing text
files is defensible; publishing a verified `AggregateRating` in JSON-LD is a different
thing, and the data behind it is real.

**#4b — the seven fleet pages are still 22% unique.** Identical to the first pass:
`/fleet/eqe-300-electric` and `/fleet/tesla-model-3` at 16%, `/fleet/group-minibus` best
at 30%, against an 80% bar for product pages. ~800 words each, but roughly four fifths is
the same prose with the car's name swapped. The hub now links to them, which makes them
easier to reach — and easier to compare.

**#7 — breadcrumbs missing on 15 pages** (was 13). Still absent from `/pricing`,
`/airport-transfers`, `/corporate`, `/hourly`, `/about`, `/contact`, `/transfers`, and now
also from all 7 locale homepages. (`/` correctly needs none.)

**#8 — 82 of 113 pages have no image** (was 74 of 98). Image *count* rose 87 → 136, but
page count rose faster. Every destination page still has none.

## New since 23 Aug

**`<html lang="en">` on every locale page.** `/es`, `/fr`, `/de`, `/ar` — all seven serve
`lang="en"`. The hreflang tags say one thing and the lang attribute says another, on
pages whose whole purpose is to be recognised as non-English. `/ar` also has no
`dir="rtl"`, though `RTL_LOCALES` in `lib/i18n.ts` already names Arabic. Small fix,
directly undercuts the locale work that just shipped.
